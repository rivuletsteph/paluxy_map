Reading notes

  • The stations are not interchangeable. 20232 (BRA) carries nitrate, orthophosphate, turbidity, DO saturation and field weather codes. 20343 (TCEQ Region) carries alkalinity, fluoride, TKN, total phosphorus, chlorophyll-a, VSS and instantaneous flow. The overlap is temperature, DO, pH, conductance, Secchi, chloride, sulfate, TSS, E. coli, flow severity and days-since-precipitation.
  • Instantaneous flow is essentially a 20343 parameter — 65 measurements there against 5 at 20232, which reports flow severity codes instead. Any flow-adjusted analysis will be one-sided.
  • Orthophosphate is 100% non-detect at both stations (83 of 83 results). Ammonia at 20343 is 88% non-detect, VSS 89%. These series describe the method, not the water.
  • E. coli geometric means shown here are period-of-record, not the seven-year assessment window TCEQ uses, and no seasonal or flow weighting is applied. Treat them as orientation, not as an assessment result.
  • Censored values are plotted at their reported limit. Greater-than values (Secchi at 20343, days-since-precipitation) are likewise plotted at the limit, which compresses the upper tail.
  • The export holds 3,130 records, all flagged Routine monitoring.
